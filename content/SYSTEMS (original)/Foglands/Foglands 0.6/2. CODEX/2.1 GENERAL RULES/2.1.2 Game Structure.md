This game creates stories of truly epic proportions. In one sitting, you might go from the minutiae of punches and parries, to winessing a century of societal changes in the Bastion you call home. For this reason, the three game modes (Trials, Journeys and Interludes) assign different meanings to concepts such as game rounds, movement or actions. Still, all three modes follow the same structure. While an overview lies below, the specifics of their application are detailed in each game mode's section.

# Rounds & Turns

A Round is the basic unit of time, a full cycle in which each part of the Scene has its Turn in the spotlight to take actions. One exchange of blows from all parties, one leg of a journey or decade of city life

## Structure of a Round

Though game modes differ in their application of this structure, the concept remains the same. The Start is where everyone moves and sometimes rushes their actions (sacrificing 1 of their 2 actions to act now). The Upkeep is where the world happens around you. Turns are where actions are taken without penalty. The End signals the completion of a Round, and is used to track effect durations.

# Actions

To no one's surprise, actions are the things someone or something does. This is a character swinging an axe as much as it is party scouting a location or a cult recruiting members in the slums.

❫ 2 actions per Turn, one of which can be Bold.
❫ 1 action when Rushing 

## Bold actions

A simple way of saying you are spending the action on a feature marked with the **bold** tag. Because you can only do this once a round, and cannot do anything Bold when you are **feared**, it is a good habit to declare the use of a **bold** action.
