Your Defence, expressed as a number, represents your ability to effortlessly avoid harm. When you are threatened with damage, you can either trust in your Defence and suffer it or spend 1 Stamina to make a Reaction roll. 

If you receive more damage in a Round than your Defence, you become overwhelmed and suffer harm, which varies for each game mode. Trials will see you Wounded or dead. In Journeys, you will accrue Fog, lose time and precious items. Meanwhile in interludes, harm represents setbacks or blows to your long-term ambitions.
