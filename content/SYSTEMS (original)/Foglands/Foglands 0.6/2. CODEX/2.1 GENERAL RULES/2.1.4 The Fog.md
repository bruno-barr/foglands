It ebbs and flows like a sea, and is everyone’s deadliest concern. It is a sentient, malevolent creature that subsumes all life and twists reality itself. Even at its thinnest, its pervasive influence can be felt as an intrusive thought, a burst of anger or sluggishness. It is malice, raw and unadulterated.

To a rare few like yourself, it grants freedom from thirst, hunger and age. Its power courses through your lungs, allowing you to perform feats beyond the reach of mundane folk, though a cost. You will find it impossible to survive long without using your gifts and accepting your curses.

The Fog’s influence over you is cyclical. First, it accumulates in your body. When you reach your limit, it lays a curse on you, dissipating in the process. Then, the cycle repeats, leaving you free to indulge in further temptation.

# Using the Fog

You accrue Fog Points (FOG) to boost your rolls, to use you dark gifts, or as a consequence of your environment. FOG is always accrued by rolling a specified die size. When you go over your FOG limit, you roll on the curse table and reset your FOG to zero.
