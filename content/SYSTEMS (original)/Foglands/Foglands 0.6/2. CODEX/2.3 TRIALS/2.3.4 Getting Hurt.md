## Armor

Each armor piece you wear informs the die size rolled when you take a wound. By default, you roll a d6, and each armour has its own die size, either d8, d10 and d12.

## Mortal Wound\*

**_A gruesome, deadly strike leaving you crawling and bleeding out._**

❫ Base movement and Move actions are reduced to Move‹1›
❫ All rolls are made with Disadv‹1›.
❫ Double Stamina costs. 

Two successful Diff‹10› Mending rolls downgrades this to a Grievous Wound. Otherwise, you will die in three rounds during Upkeep. 

## Serious wound

_A deep wound that turns life-threatening before the day is over._

❫ Double Stamina costs. 
❫ All rolls are made with Disadv‹1›. 

Anyone can roll Mending with Spark, diff‹10› to stabilize the wound and remove the Disadv‹1›. If the wound isn’t stabilized before switching to a Journey, roll 1d6 every upkeep. On a 1, the wound upgrades to Mortal. In Journeys, the mending roll can be attempted once per Round with the Recovery action.

## Moderate wound

_More than enough to stop fighting, if you had a choice…_

❫ Double Stamina costs. 

## Light Wound

_You’ll live, and earn a new scar for your troubles._

❫ No mechanical effect. Minor wounds heal at the end of the Scene.
