Trials are tense affairs, where distance and positioning can mean life or death. Trials are always played with maps, which you are free to measure in hexes, squares or any unit of measurement that makes the players happy. 

With this in mind, movement and ranges are always described as a number of units and written as Move‹?› and Range‹?›, where ‹?› is the number of units available.

# Base Movement

Unless specified otherwise, the base movement for every creature is Move‹8› and has no action cost. Base movement must be used at Round Start (see next page).

## Falling prone & other shameful fumbles

Unless otherwise specified, it costs Move‹1› to recover from falling prone, getting stuck, losing your grip, or any such fumble or mishap. Cumulative mishaps require cumulative Movevement costs.
