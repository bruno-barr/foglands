During Trials, actions are simple concepts that are quick to explain and execute. You can perform 2 actions each Round on your party's Turn, or a single action if you choose to Rush (acting during Round Start). No matter what, only 1 action each Round can be Bold.

❫ Move. Immediately Move‹4›. Unused units of movement are lost right after the action is resolved.

❫ Attack. If this is the second attack you make this turn, it costs 1 Stamina. Attacks that do not require you to pay an action cost are exempt from this rule.

❫ Techniques: Additional effects that can be tacked onto an attack. Unlocked by investing in skills. 

❫ Cast a spell. If this is the second spell you cast this turn, you roll with Disadv‹1›. A cast that does not require you to pay an action cost is  exempt from this rule.)

❫ Perform a simple object interaction: change weapons, drink a potion, activate a simple special item. A complex interaction might require 2 actions (At Host's discretion).

❫ Any other action of similar complexity not listed here (at Host’s discretion).

# Bold action

A simple way of saying you are spending the action on a feature marked with the Bold tag. Because you can only do so once a Round, and cannot do anything Bold when you are Feared, it is a good habit to declare the use of a  Bold action. 

❫ Perform a Bold Feat

❫ Use a Dark Gift

❫ Cast a spellword or an Edict

❫ Bargain with the Host - at times where you are trying to do slightly too much for the rules, or something not strictly defined by them, you can offer your Bold usage for the round as extra paiment to sweeten the deal with your Host. In such cases, the Host has the final say.
