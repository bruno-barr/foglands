**I.** A Journey begins when Outcasts leave a Bastion and only ends when they return to a Bastion, no matter how many Trials and Interludes occur in between.

**II.** Outcasts choose an Island as destination and decide whether or not they acquire a travel chart. Doing so allows Outcasts to learn beforehand about the Islands, biomes and the order in which they are encountered. More importantly, it makes certain the total length of the Journey. 

**III.** Outcasts Delve from Island to Island until they land on their destination. When in the Near Grey, each Island can be reached with 1 Delve. In the Far and Wild Grey, that number goes up to 2 and 3 respectively. 

> [!Example]
> A Journey taking you to the Near, then Far Grey means you clear 3 Delves to reach 2 Islands total

**IV.** The Islands in the Journey are drawn at random, and so are the biomes the Outcasts travel through. Refer to the worldmap  and chart a line between departure and destination. For each time the Outcasts cross fog zones (Near, Far, Wild), add 1 Island to the Journey. Roll these Islands from their appropriate tables on the next few pages.

# Travel charts

Your primary option is to compile a travel chart with your whole group’s combined effort. Each Outcast declares the manner in which they attempt to find information, then the Host requests appropriate skill rolls (the Difficulty of which is relative to the actions undertaken) or shifts the game to a Trial when needed.

If the total number of successes is equal to or greater than the number of Islands, the chart is acquired. For these rolls, each degree of Impact counts as a success. The Host reveals the Islands drawn, the types of Biomes and the Calamities on the way, if any. Upon failure, another option must be used.

Your second option is to acquire a chart from the mysterious Charting Guilds. They don’t come cheap, but their accuracy is never in question. Not only do you learn about all Islands and biomes on your path, but also notable unavoidable dangers.

## Leaving without a chart

 Good luck! Your third option isn't really one at all. Islands are rolled as you reach them. When you do, flip a coin. If you lose the toss, you cannot move to another fog zone and must go through another island, where the process repeats until Outcasts win a coin toss.
