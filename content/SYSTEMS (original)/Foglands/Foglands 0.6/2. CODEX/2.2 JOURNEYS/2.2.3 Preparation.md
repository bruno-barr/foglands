# Preparation

There are man things that need to be done to survive a Journey into the fogsea. The more the Outcasts know about the path ahead, the easier it will be to come up with the appropriate solutions. 

The considerations are many; fuel for light sources, clothes for all climates, carts,  porting gear and food rations for hirelings, emergency consumables to heal, slow down and distract… Outcasts likely will run out of money before long. Indeed, the difference experience makes is in what one chooses to prioritize.

Each biome presents its own set of environmental dangers, for which the Free Kins have found creative answers over the centuries. Many items and consumables are flexible enough to offer partial solutions to various hurdles, while others, more rare and expensive, offer a surefire solution to specifc dangers.

## Armors and Weapons

The careful Outcast always packs a diversity of weapons and selects its equipment strategically. Armor is heavy, weapons may break, and while some fog spawns are easily stabbed, others are better battered cracked open. A stronger item isn’t better if it weighs you down or costs half your budget.

## Tools & Consumables

As essential to one’s survival as plate and blade, if not more so. As a group, Outcasts should have at least one emergency solution to all types of dangers (wounds, mental asaults, natural obstacles, the Fog), and one item dedicated to countering a specific biome’s dangers.

> [!Example]
> bandages and clotting sap are universally useful for wounds, while mucus salves specifically help counter the poisonous spores of the wetlands.

## Transport & Hired Hands

Animals react to the fog-touched with primal fear, to the point where Outcasts are unable to ride a mount or even guide pack animals. To travel with any amount of heavy load or drawn carriages, they must therefore hire people to do it for them. This comes with its own challenges, especially when heading on a Journey that steps into the Wild Grey.
