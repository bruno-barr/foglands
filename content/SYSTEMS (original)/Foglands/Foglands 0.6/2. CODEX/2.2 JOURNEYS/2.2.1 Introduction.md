Forcing someone into the Fog without information or equipment is seen as attempted murder by most settlements, and is levied as punishment for severe crimes. To survive the ever-changing fog currents, one must chart convoluted paths through a number of Islands, anchor points that stand defiantly against the fogsea. 

The Journey is a game mode that focuses on telling the story of the Outcasts' travels from a bird’s eye view. You will resolve days, months or years of travel with a few dice rolls, and flow in and out of dialogs as the story emerges. It is akin to gamifying a collective campfire tale, by adding rules to resolve what happens at each intersection in the story. 

You embark on a Journey as soon as you leave the low-fog areas surrounding all bastions, travelling through the Near, the Far, and the Wild Grey. A Journey only ends when you return to a bastion, no matter how many Trials or Interludes you experience in between.

## Near Grey

The Near Grey lays just outside the Bastions’ direct area of influence. The fog is thin and its currents are mostly slow and predictable. Fog Spawns are uncommon, settlements are semi-nomadic and large in scale, and the authority of the nearest Bastion is still somewhat recognized.

## Far Grey

The Far Grey is where civilization dies. With the massive increase in fog density comes a slew of new dangers. Life here is brutal, such that even hardened tribal bands only cross into the Far Grey as a last resort. A few settlements exist, about which we know precious little. Elves are everywhere and Fog Spawns are Common. Most bastions have replaced the dealth penalty with Far Grey duty.

**Wild Grey**

The Wild Grey covers most of the world. Here, the sun is a forgettable dot and warmth is a dream. The Fog density is absolute and finding your way is a matter of faith, intuition or sensitivity to the occult. Senses cannot be trusted, logic doesn’t exist, and the land will try to eat you before the countless Fog Spawns do. The old world’s greatest secrets and treasures lay there, waiting for you.

## Worldmap

In a land where cities vanish and landscapes shift, a worldmap serves a vastly different purpose. Much like the world it represents, your map makes no attempt to reason with distance or scale. Instead, it outlines fog densities (near, far, wild) and the general location of Bastions, Islands and Calamities.

## Islands

Islands are small havens of hope in the fogsea. They emerge cyclically, remaining inhabitable for a few hundred years at most before being swallowed again. Not all Islands are equal, however. Some are objectively harder to access than others, for no other reason than the fog deciding it be so. 

**Bastions**

Bastions are a type of Island that never disappear. They  are sprawling city-states, the last refuges of civilization against the fog’s bottomless hunger. Cities so old they might as well be eternal, old even when the world fell. Bastions are unique for their miraculous ability to thin the fog a few leagues around them, like candle-smoke in the wind. How? Why?

# Delves

A Delve is the common name for a collection practicable paths towards an Island. In play, it is represented by a point-crawl map, where each point corresponds to the high and low points in your Journey. Each contains something interesting like dangers, treasures and or story elements. The symbol on each point marks what is known about it

**Elf:** 
This location is known to contain monsters.

**Mountain:** 
This location contains treacherous terrain or hazards.

**Fog:** 
This location contains unpredictable currents and fog densities. Expect occult phenomenons. Highly dangerous.

**Dice:** 
Outcasts know nothing about this place. The Host draws or rolls from the list. The Near, Far and Wild Grey each have their own list.
