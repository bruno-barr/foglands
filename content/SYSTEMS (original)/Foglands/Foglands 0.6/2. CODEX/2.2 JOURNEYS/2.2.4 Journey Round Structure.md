# Structure of a Round

While in a Delve, the party of Outcasts moves and performs actions as a single unit. 

**Start**\
_Upkeep_\
**Turns**\
**End**

## Round Start

All creatures move. One Outcast rolls  Fogdelving (Spark) for the whole party against the Delve’s Difficulty. For each degree of Success, the party can move 1 Point on the Delve. If the roll fails, the Outcasts still move 1 Point, but take twice as long to do so.

```
MOVE‹2› FOR A MAJOR SUCCESS.  
MOVE ‹3› FOR A CRITICAL SUCCESS
```

## Rushing

During Round Start, the Outcasts can sacrifice one of their Round’s two actions to _act now._ The act of **rushing** also claws back the time lost on a failed Fogdelving roll.

## Upkeep

The Upkeep is where the world happens. Travel time is updated here The contents of the Point you reached unfold, and recurring effects trigger (wounds, poisons, etc).

## Turns

This is where the Outcast party and the pursuers (if any) perform two actions (if they didn’t **rush** during Round Start).

## Round End

This is where effects and events entering in their last Round of duration will end. 

# Actions

- **Scout Ahead -** One of the Outcasts explores an upcoming Point while the others rest. Cannot be part of another action this Round.

```
The scouter  cannot recover,  craft/repair or scavenge for the round
```

- **Recovery -** Outcasts gain 1 Stamina, lose 1 Fog, and can roll Mending with Spark, diff‹10› to stabilize a Serious wound or remove a Moderate wound. 
- **Crafting/Repair -** Outcasts can each make a single roll towards crafting or reparing an item.
- **Scavenge -** Outcasts can each make a Survival roll agaisnt the Delve’s Difficulty.
