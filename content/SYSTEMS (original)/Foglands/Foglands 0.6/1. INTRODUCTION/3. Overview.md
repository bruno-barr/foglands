Foglands is a game of impossible journeys, sacrifice and survival, one that asks if you would rather escape your prison or rule over it.

In it, players act either as cursed Outcasts, or as Host. The Outcasts explore the world’s vastness and depth in search of freedom, power, answers, riches, justice and revenge. The single Host makes the Foglands come alive and acts as referee. 

Cursed One! As you travel, negotiate and fight, you will find that this game encourages you to raise the stakes. It happily enables your greed and risk-taking, and feeds upon your desperation not to lose. 

Host! You too are meant to take risks. While you aren’t playing to make the other loses, the game will be made better if you prepare twisted dilemmas, nail-biting challenges and terrifying opponents. Very few stories end well in the Foglands, but you are there to ensure all are memorable.

# Setting

At the center of the world of Mellus lies a prison of sentient fog. To its inhabitants, it might as well be the whole world, one in which reality bends and twist constantly. This pallid fogsea eats away at heat and light, corrupts nature and minds, and unleashes its monstrous spawns upon the land.  And yet, civilization survives. 

Nine Bastions stand. Nine ageless city-states, in which the free Kins find shelther and sometimes even hope. Hope to see a clear sky, hope to reach the outside, hope to vanquish the horrors and shine a bright light on the secrets of old.**Hurry! I’m READING in the shop!**

- The game shifts between three game modes: Journeys, Moments and Interludes. Each focuses on a different scope and scale of storytelling. Each adds its own twist to the general rules.
- Travel through the foglands in a constantly evolving point-crawl. These Journeys are epic in scale, not unlike the myths of Antiquity. 
- Time is a currency, player-characters don’t age and leveling-up causes vast time skips. See the world change around you.
- Combat is fast, brutal and tactical. It often isn’t the only or best solution but hey, combat is fun!
- Customize the game’s difficulty by choosing from a list of starting scenarios.

# Mechanics

- Foglands requires dice with four, six, eight, ten, twelve and twenty sides, commonly referred to as d4, d6, d8, d10, d12 and d20. 
- A d12 + d? roll-over system. The higher result is compared to the Difficulty while the other determines the roll’s Impact (degree of success). That's it. Everything, from weapon damage to spells or skills, is handled in with that single roll.
- Accrue FOG (fog points) to boost skill rolls and use dark powers. Go over your fog limit and get cursed, unlocking stronger gifts in the process.
- Your character evolves through a class-based system with horizontal progression. Decide which of your Path (class), Aspects or Skills define your character more.+
