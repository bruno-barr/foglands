## This land is a prison of fog,

The baleful sun lights a land as cold, damp and foul as its people. Fire is sacred, steel is a treasure, and dryness and safety are a luxury. Whether in the bastion-states or out in the Grey, life is a currency. Give an arm to take a head, trade a friend for a fortune, lend your soul to borrow greatness.

## savage and Wonderful!

There are few dawns without storms or dusks without wonders. Greedy raiders, flesh-hungry elves and deadly fog spawns infest the land and yet, every lush hill and deep valley, every wild grassland, mountain and cave teems with impossible sights and otherwordly treasures.

## Something is eating away at the world...

It ebbs and flows like a sea, and is everyone’s deadliest concern. It is a sentient, malevolent entity that subsumes all life and twists reality itself. Even at its thinnest, its pervasive influence can be felt as rash decisions and intrusive thoughts. It is malice, raw and unadulterated.

## and your gods cannot save you!

The gods' only presence lies in broken temples and tribal traditions. Only two lesser powers remain—the Kindler and the Flame—and even they grow weaker by the year. By gifting power to their followers, they have made their last gambit. Meanwhile in the Bastions, the rulers enjoy their own worship, through miracles or brutal repression. Worshipers of the Fog also exist, and they are invariably insane.

## Yet, there is still is hope!

All nine Bastions choke in their grasp of an immortal ruler-once-hero… All but one! Deephaven stands alone, free and unbowed. Though it is flawed and corrupt, all is forgiven to the last city to kindle hope.

# Three Shades of Play

You will experience the Foglands in three ways, each flowing differently and focusing on a unique aspect of the story. See them as three ways to  interact with the general rules, rather than a giant three-headed beast of complexity.

## Journeys

Journeys are all about travels through the fog and below the earth. They abstract the small details to focus on your key decisions and their impact on your travels. They might compress weeks, months or years with every roll of the dice, until you reach your destination or die trying. 

Outcasts more interested in world exploration, or those who like the feel of old knight-errant tales, will spend a lot of time in Journey mode. When something too complex or dangerous occurs, you interrupt a Journey to get in a Trial. 

## **Trials** 

Trials are your movie scenes. They are the moments where the exact order and outcome of unfolding events is crucial. The game switches to a Trial when time is of the essence or your life is at risk. Outcasts who like fighting, exploring ruins or pulling off heists would all spend a lot of time resolving Trials.

## **Interludes**

Interludes are concerned with life outside of the Journeys and Trials. They represent what might be called downtime between each high point. This is where you heal and rebuild, hire, scheme and relax. Outcasts can spend a surprising amount of time in Interludes if they are interested in meticulous planning and crafting (p?), political or mercantile intrigue, or if they choose to establish an organization.
